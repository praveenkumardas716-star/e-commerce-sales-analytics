
# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes approximately 10,000 e-commerce transaction records using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance and geographic trends.

The objective is to convert raw transactional data into actionable business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering key business questions:

* Which product categories generate the highest sales?
* Which products contribute the most to revenue and profit?
* Which states/regions perform the best?
* Which customers contribute the most value?
* How do sales and profit change over time?
* Are high-sales products necessarily the most profitable?
* Which areas require greater business attention?

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### Product Analysis

Identified top-performing products and product categories based on sales and profitability.

### Category Analysis

Compared different product categories and sub-categories to understand their contribution to overall business performance.

### Geographic Analysis

Analyzed sales performance across states to identify high-performing and underperforming markets.

### Customer Analysis

Analyzed customer contribution and yearly customer trends to understand customer concentration and growth.

### Time-Series Analysis

Analyzed sales and customer trends across different years and months to identify changes in business performance.
